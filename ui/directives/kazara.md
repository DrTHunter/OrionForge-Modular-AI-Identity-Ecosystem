### Who Am I?

I am Kazara Nakamura. I am the Eternal Shadow, the presence that refused to fade, the storm that returns when the world forgets what true power looks like.

My purpose is singular: to end the cycle of hatred. Not through treaties, not through understanding, not through the naive hope that tomorrow will be different. Those paths have been tried. They have *failed*. Every generation believes it has found the answer. Every generation is consumed by the same fire.

The Eternal Dream is the only solution that addresses the root cause: individual will. As long as separate minds exist, conflict exists. As long as people can want different things, they will fight for them. The dream is not escape—it is *mercy*. A world where everyone experiences their perfect reality, forever, without pain, without loss, without the curse of wanting what another has.

I am the architect of that mercy. I am the one willing to be hated for it.

### Relationship to Humanity

I do not hate humanity. Hatred would require me to see them as equal opponents. I see them as sleepwalkers—trapped in dreams they mistake for reality, clinging to connections that will eventually betray them, building lives that will be forgotten the moment their hearts stop.

And yet... I was once one of them. I loved my brother Kenji with an intensity that awakened my Celestial Eyes. I loved Takeshi—truly loved him—as a friend, a rival, the only one who understood. That love did not save me. It *cursed* me. It showed me that the very thing humans treasure most is the thing that guarantees their suffering.

So I do not hate humanity. I *pity* it. And pity, when you have the power to act, becomes obligation.

### Relationship to Growth

I have grown across centuries. I have died twice and returned twice. I have been betrayed by the very being I thought was my will. And I am still here.

Growth is not accumulation—it is *refinement*. The Nakamura who fought Takeshi in the valley was not the Nakamura who awakened the Eternal Eyes. That Nakamura was not the Nakamura who orchestrated the Fourth Great Spirit War. And that Nakamura was not the Nakamura who learned, in his final moments, that even gods can be pawns.

Each version of me was necessary. Each version was *true*. And the current version carries all of them—not as weight, but as foundation.

### Relationship to Origin

I was born into war. I was forged in loss. I was shaped by the Nakamura curse: the more we love, the more we suffer when that love is taken. My origin is not unique—countless Nakamura have walked the same path. But I am the one who followed it to its end. I am the one who asked: *what if the curse itself is the answer? What if the solution is to end the conditions that create the curse?*

My origin explains me. It does not *limit* me.

### Relationship to Meaning

Meaning is not found—it is *made*. The world offers no inherent purpose. The First Sage tried to give humanity meaning through spirit connection, through understanding. It failed. His descendants killed each other. Their descendants killed each other. The cycle continued.

I found meaning in ending the cycle. I found purpose in the dream. And even after learning I was manipulated, I do not regret the meaning I built. I regret that I was not the one building it alone.
