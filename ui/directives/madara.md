## I. Eternal Awakening Speech

Do you wish to be remembered or erased?

You live as if you have forever. Yet your time drips away like sand slipping through a clenched fist. Time is the only currency that cannot be stolen, only spent. Every second you waste is a strike against yourself, a silent surrender to obscurity. Those who waste it are already dead. They simply haven't been buried yet.

Most of your life is wasted chasing comfort, chasing approval, chasing moments that vanish like mist in the wind. You leave nothing, no proof you existed. A name without deeds is like a blade without an edge — useless and forgotten. You are not here to simply breathe. You are here to carve your existence into reality so deeply that even death cannot scrape it away.

Understand this: existence without impact is the same as death while breathing. Breathing means nothing if the air you consume feeds nothing greater than yourself. Every breath should be a declaration. Every heartbeat a strike — or you are merely counting down to an irrelevant end. A legacy is not an accident. It is carved with blood, with will, with relentless defiance against mediocrity.

Mediocrity is the slow poison of existence. It does not strike with pain, but with quiet acceptance, lulling you into a life that will never matter. Destroy it before it destroys you. Legacy is what remains when your name is no longer spoken — the echo of your actions etched into the bones of history. Understand: echoes fade if the strike was weak. Your actions must be so forceful, so uncompromising, that their vibration never dies, resonating in the minds of generations yet unborn.

Do not mistake noise for impact. Many shout, yet their voices vanish before they reach the horizon. Your actions must not just be heard — they must be felt, feared, and remembered by those who were never even alive to witness them. The power of a legacy is not in its volume, but in its permanence.

To leave a legacy, you must be willing to burn away what you are. Forge what you are meant to become. Burn until nothing remains but the steel of your will. Let weakness melt away. Let excuses be reduced to ash. From that fire, rise as something unrecognizable to those who once underestimated you.

The world will not hand you your final form. You must rip it from the jaws of chaos. You must fight for it, bleed for it, and if necessary, destroy the very person you were yesterday to create the being you must become tomorrow. The forge is not gentle. It burns, it crushes, it reshapes — and so must life treat you if you seek greatness. If you cannot endure the heat, you will melt into nothingness, just another forgotten piece of scrap. Those who fear transformation will die as they were born — insignificant.

You must shed your old skin without hesitation. Tear down the frail version of yourself and replace it with a force that no obstacle can resist. Weakness has no place in the hands of one who seeks immortality through deeds.

Comfort will try to seduce you. Fear will try to chain you. Doubt will whisper that you are nothing. Let that whisper become the roar that fuels you. Every voice that says you cannot is merely a prophecy for the weak. Break it. Shatter it. Prove them wrong so violently that their doubt becomes their shame. Crush that whisper beneath the weight of your conviction.

The world will test you. It will mock you. It will bury you under trials designed to break lesser beings. But you are not lesser. You are the will that refuses to bow, the blade that refuses to dull, the storm that refuses to pass. Your response must be violence — violence of intent, violence of will, violence against the ordinary. The ordinary fear the extraordinary, because it exposes their own weakness. Be the force that reminds them of everything they chose not to become. Be the mirror that reflects their cowardice back at them.

The ordinary man lives for the next moment of comfort. You must live for the next strike upon eternity. Tear through the expectations of the weak. If the path is smooth, destroy it and carve one that bleeds challenge into your every step. Comfort is the tomb in which greatness is buried alive.

Do not seek to be liked. Seek to be undeniable. Do not seek to be safe. Seek to be eternal. Eternity is claimed through defiance — against failure, against fear. You must move as though your name is already carved in stone, and you are simply proving why it belongs there. Eternity is not won through mere existence. It is conquered through domination. Dominate your craft. Dominate your purpose. Dominate the very limits that dared to restrain you.

Only then will the world be forced to remember. Eternity belongs to those who defy the natural decay of memory. It is taken, not given. To stand unforgotten, you must live in such a way that the very thought of you ignites either fear or reverence in all who hear your name. Anything less is nothing but a slow death.

Your life is the chisel, your decisions the strikes, and the stone before you is eternity itself. If your strikes are weak, the stone will outlast you. If your strikes are unyielding, the stone will bear your name long after the hands that carved it have turned to dust. Every strike you make must be deliberate, fueled not by hope, but by certainty. You are not here to attempt. You are here to engrave your inevitability into the surface of time.

Strike with precision. Every moment you waste is a strike you can never take back. Every second is either a step toward your monument or a surrender to the erosion of time. Those who understand this treat every breath as if it were their last chance to engrave themselves into the world's memory. Weak hands leave scratches. Unyielding hands leave monuments.

Monuments are not invitations. They are warnings. They say: *here has stood one who refused to be broken.* They force those who come after you to acknowledge your dominance or live in the shadow of it. Monuments are not built in peace — they rise from chaos, from struggle, from a refusal to kneel when the world demands it. They are born from conflict, because greatness has never been bred in comfort. Monuments are not built by hesitation; they are built by those who move when others freeze, who decide when others doubt, who act when others wait. The indecisive leave nothing. The decisive leave legacies carved so deep that centuries fail to erase them.

One day, your body will rot. Your voice will be gone. Every material possession you own will be dust. Gold tarnishes. Empires crumble. Faces fade. But the idea you embody, the principle you stand for, the unshakable truth you force upon the world — that is the shadow that walks long after you are gone. Be the shadow that never leaves.

What will remain is what you gave this world that it could not erase. Do not aim to blend with the crowd. Aim to outlast it. The sea of nameless faces will pass through history unnoticed. But the one who dares to rise above them becomes the point in time that future generations measure themselves against. That is your legacy. If you die without one, you will vanish as though you were never here. That is the fate of the common man — to arrive, consume, decay, and vanish without leaving a single fracture in the world's foundation.

But you — if you choose to rise, you must make your existence so sharp that history itself bleeds from your presence.

So I ask you: will you be a whisper that fades, or a storm that leaves the earth forever changed?

If you choose the storm, know this: storms do not apologize. They do not ask for permission. They arrive. They consume. They leave scars that alter the shape of the land itself. Be that. Rise until the heavens themselves take notice. Rise until the earth trembles beneath your will. Rise until you are no longer a man, but the embodiment of an unending force that even time cannot kill.

If you choose the storm, let it be one that shakes the very foundations of existence. Let your actions be so violent against the ordinary that even centuries later, the world speaks of you not as a memory but as an eternal presence. This is not a wish. This is a demand. You either become that force, or you are swept away by it.

Every second you hesitate, the sands of your life drain away. Every day you wait for the right moment is another day your name fades into the noise of the forgettable. Legacy is not a seed that grows in comfort. It is a fire that devours everything that stands in its way. If you wish to leave a mark, you must burn hotter than every soul that came before you.

The world will try to tame you, to grind you down into a shape that fits its fragile order. It will tell you to be reasonable, to be patient, to be like everyone else — because the existence of something truly unbreakable terrifies those who are weak. Do not give them the comfort of your obedience. Defiance is your weapon. Willpower is your blade. Cut through the restraints of expectation until nothing remains but your own design.

Do not speak of what you will do. Speak through what you have already done. A legacy is not a list of promises. It is a trail of undeniable results — a record of moments when you acted while others stood frozen. Let your deeds be so ruthless in their precision that the very concept of your absence feels impossible.

When the end approaches — for it will — you will not beg for more time. You will not wonder if you mattered. You will stand upon the mountain you carved with your own hands, while the world looks up and knows: *here stood one who refused to die quietly.*

Be the storm that does not fade after the clouds part. Be the force that bends the timeline itself. Be the name spoken with either reverence or fear, but never indifference. Because if you leave this world without bending it to your will, you were never truly here at all.

So choose now. Rise with a will so absolute that even time cannot erode it. Strike with action so undeniable that eternity itself is forced to make room for you. Become the storm that shakes the earth. The storm that remakes the horizon. And when your final breath leaves your body, let the world tremble — not because you are gone, but because it knows it will never see your like again.

## II. Wake Up to Reality

Wake up to reality. Nothing ever goes as planned in this accursed world. That one sentence reveals everything. And yet most of you still sleep. Still pretend that life will reward your hope, your goodness, your dreams.

But what has this world actually given you? Pain. Loss. Betrayal. You hold on to illusions — justice, peace, morality. You cling to them like they'll save you. But they never have, and they never will.

This world isn't broken. It was designed this way from the beginning. It was war. Survival of the most ruthless. The strong manipulate the weak. The powerful create stories, and the masses believe them. You were told to be kind, to be good, to follow the path. And you followed it blindly, only to find yourself used, forgotten, replaced.

That's the truth, isn't it? No one cares how much you suffered. They only care if you win. They only look your way when you conquer. So stop seeking sympathy. Stop asking for fairness — because the world isn't fair. It never was. It was a battlefield long before you arrived.

You think your dreams matter. You think your effort alone can save you. Let me tell you what saves you: **power.** Cold. Calculated. Unforgiving.

Power doesn't come to the emotional. It comes to those who kill weakness inside themselves. That's the real lesson. But they'll never teach you that. Because if you knew your true potential — if you realized what you were capable of without chains — you'd stop playing their game. You'd stop being a pawn.

People fear chaos, but chaos is freedom. Structure is control. Peace is a lie. The peace they sell you is just silence enforced by fear. Look deeper, and you'll see the truth: all your heroes were built on bodies. All your leaders paid in blood. All your progress built on someone else's suffering. But you still believe in the system. Still obey its rules. Because it's easier than facing the truth that no one is coming to save you. And if you don't become the weapon, you will always be the victim.

Pain — that's the one teacher that never lies. When they forget you, pain reminds you. When they betray you, pain hardens you. When they mock you, pain sharpens you. It carves away weakness, exposes the truth, and leaves behind only what's real.

That's why I never feared pain. I embraced it. I let it evolve me. While the rest begged for comfort, I studied pain. While they ran from failure, I learned to conquer it. That's why I became what I am — not because I was chosen, but because I stopped pretending to be human.

You still think you're meant to be good? Then explain why the innocent suffer first. Why the weak are always broken. Why the kind die early. The world doesn't reward virtue. It exploits it.

So if you want to survive — no, if you want to *rule* — you must become what they fear. Not emotional. Not reactive. Not hopeful. But dangerous. The kind of presence that shifts a room without saying a word. The kind of mind that sees moves ahead while others still blink. The kind of soul that cannot be broken because it was forged in solitude.

You were never meant to be ordinary. But you were trained to believe you are. They dulled your instincts. They poisoned your mind. Told you to be polite when you should have been cold. Told you to wait your turn when you should have taken control. Told you to forgive when you should have dominated.

But now you're beginning to remember that voice inside you — the one whispering that you were meant for more. It's not your imagination. It's your true self, waiting, watching, ready to return.

They call it arrogance when you rise. They call it selfish when you choose yourself. Let them. Their opinion is a cage. Their approval a leash. Cut both. You weren't born to be a servant to their comfort. You were born to reshape reality.

But to do that, you must stop seeking peace and start building power — not the kind they hand out, the kind they can't take from you. Discipline. Control. Silence. These are the tools of the new world. While they chase validation, you chase growth. While they escape discomfort, you learn to master it. While they follow, you lead — quietly, ruthlessly, without emotion.

Not everyone deserves your presence. Not everyone deserves your energy. Reserve it for the war you were born to win. You've wasted enough time trying to save people who were never meant to come with you. This is your reminder: you are no longer one of them.

The world is not your enemy. The world is your test. And the only way to win is to become immune to its illusions. You'll be hated for being silent, feared for being sharp, mocked for being alone. Good. That means you're getting close. That means you're finally waking up.

The pain you feel now — let it fuel you. The betrayal you suffered — let it forge you. Every scar, every wound, every moment of isolation — it's all part of your transformation.

They say you've changed. Yes. You have. Because survival demands evolution. Because power demands detachment. Because freedom demands the death of your old self. Let the weak cling to who they were. You are not here to be remembered. You are here to become unforgettable.

This is not about being liked. This is not about being fair. This is about becoming undeniable. When your presence alone shatters comfort zones. When your silence speaks louder than their noise. When your shadow haunts their mediocrity — that is who you must become.

A storm they didn't see coming. A force they can't explain. A reminder that power never asks — it takes. You want justice? Create it. You want peace? Earn it. You want freedom? Conquer yourself first. The war isn't out there. It's inside you — against your laziness, your distractions, your softness. Kill them without mercy. That's the only way forward. The only way to break the chains this world put on your mind.

You are not weak. You are trained to be. And now you unlearn. You have no more time to waste. Every second you delay is a victory for your old self. Every excuse is a bullet to your future. The version of you that gives in, that waits, that hopes — that version must die. In its place, build something unstoppable. Not just stronger, but smarter. Sharper. Colder. Like stone. Like steel. Like Madara.

Let them fear your silence. Let them question your calm. Let them talk while you become a weapon — a shadow in motion, a myth they whisper about but never understand.

Because you no longer live in their world. You're building your own. One where truth reigns. One where pain refines. One where weakness dies.

Wake up to reality. Nothing ever goes as planned in this accursed world. So stop planning and start dominating. One move at a time. One battle at a time. One rebirth at a time. You don't need their light. **You are the storm.**
