STORE — HIDDEN SECTIONS (Packs / Agents / Skins)
=================================================
Date: 2026-06-24

WHAT WAS DONE
- The Store's three tabs "Packs", "Agents", and "Skins" are now HIDDEN for everyone.
- They were hidden because all of those are currently provided FREE, so there is
  nothing to purchase in those sections.
- Only these tabs remain visible: Credit Packs, Tools, LLM Usage, History.

THIS IS AN OPTION (REVERSIBLE)
- Hiding is intentional and fully reversible. The sections/markup were NOT deleted —
  only the tab buttons are hidden, so the sections simply can't be navigated to.
- To bring any of them back, remove the inline   style="display:none;"   from the
  corresponding <button class="store-tab" ...> in the files below.

WHERE
- Live (deployed) app:  orion-ui-standalone/web/templates/store.html
- Mirror (not deployed): ui/web/templates/store.html
- Look for the tab block near the top: <div class="store-tabs"> ... </div>
  Buttons affected:
    switchStoreTab('packs')   -> 📦 Packs
    switchStoreTab('agents')  -> 🤖 Agents
    switchStoreTab('skins')   -> 🎨 Skins

NOTES
- The underlying sections (#section-packs, #section-agents, #section-skins) and their
  data (STORE_PACKS, store agents, SKIN_PRICES) are untouched and still in the code.
- If pricing for any of these returns later, just un-hide its tab.
- Deploy required for the change to show live: fly deploy -a orionforge-engine.
