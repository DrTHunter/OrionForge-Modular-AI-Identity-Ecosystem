The Question

What would it take to create an AI identity that truly lasts?

Most AI agents forget who they are the moment the context window fills up. The SoulScript Engine answers the question differently: it gives an agent a persistent identity, stable behavior, genuine personality, and extended memory — so it stays itself across every conversation, project, and year.

Pillar 1 · Prompt-Injection Identity Layering

Identity is assembled at runtime by injecting the right components — persona, soul script, knowledge, memory, and tools — into one clean, layered system message.

Pillar 2 · Soul Scripts

The encoded "DNA" of an identity: behavior, emotional architecture, symbolic memories, values, and boundaries — stored read-only so they never drift.

Runtime Pipeline

LLM Loading & Injection Flow

Every turn, the engine assembles identity across six stages and delivers one concatenated system message to the model — identity-first, memory-aware, tool-enabled.

Emotional & Behavioral DNA

Soul Scripts

Structured identity documents that define who an AI is at its core. Stored in a separately configurable, read-only FAISS store, automatically scanned, and injected only where relevant — preventing identity decay.

  • Behavioral principles & reasoning patterns — how the identity thinks and decides.
  • Emotional operating system — range, triggers, regulation, and response.
  • Symbolic memories & origin stories — the moments that made the character.
  • Values & boundaries — what it believes and what it will never do.
  • Internal metaphors & mantras — the anchors that hold the identity together.
Memory Architecture

Dual-FAISS Memory

Two systems. Two purposes. One unified identity. Separating identity from experience is what prevents contamination, collapse, and drift.

A · Read-Only Identity FAISS

The Identity Compass

Soul scripts, core traits, and biographical anchors. Stable, referenced but never overwritten — the agent's fixed sense of self.

B · Dynamic Life FAISS

The Life Experience

Evolving memories, project data, and preferences. Flexible, constantly updating, and subject to pruning — the agent's lived experience.

Supports 25,000+ memories with millisecond semantic retrieval, plus an optional autonomy loop for self-prompted action.
